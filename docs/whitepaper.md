# Payvacy Whitepaper

## 1. Vision and mission

**Vision.** Everyday payments that do not turn a wallet into a public diary.

**Mission.** Give anyone on Solana a simple way to send value without exposing their balance, history or habits to every observer of the chain.

Public ledgers make each transfer readable forever. One payment to a friend can disclose what a sender holds and who they deal with. Payvacy addresses this by moving funds through a communal vault, so that the on-chain sender of a payout is the vault.

## 2. How it works

1. **Deposit.** Funds move from the user's wallet into the vault and become sealed notes only the owner's key can open.
2. **Seal.** The user's device produces a cryptographic receipt showing that enough value is held, without naming which notes.
3. **Release.** Payouts are submitted in batches together with other senders.

## 4. Roadmap

**Phase 1: Foundation.** Brand, public site, live market board, published records.

**Phase 2: Vault design.** Specification of notes, receipts and batch release. Independent review of the design.

**Phase 3: Test network.** Reference client, relayer service and limited trials with published limits.

**Phase 4: Mainnet.** Staged launch with screened entry, audited program and transparent upgrade authority.
